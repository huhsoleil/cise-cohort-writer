## 3. CiSE formal requirements relevant to cohort studies

- Original Article: structured abstract up to 250 words; up to 30 references; no fixed limit on text or tables/figures (template suggests 5,000 words and 10 tables/figures).
- Reporting guidelines are *recommended* (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA via EQUATOR). The skill treats STROBE as mandatory and attaches a completed checklist.
- References: numbered in order of first citation, in square brackets [ ]; list all authors if 6 or fewer, otherwise first 3 + "et al."; NLM journal abbreviations; omit repeated page digits (e.g., 1226-34).
- Tables: numbered by order of mention; abbreviations defined in footnotes; footnote symbols a), b), c) or *P<0.05, **P<0.01, ***P<0.001; self-explanatory.
- Figures: separate files (EPS/TIFF/PDF); 300 dpi colour/halftone, 600 dpi greyscale, 1,200 dpi line art; legends after the last table.
- AI policy (ICMJE-based): AI cannot be an author; authors are responsible for AI output; disclose tool, model name, version, manufacturer and role in Methods or Acknowledgments (prompts where relevant); AI-generated images/videos are not allowed unless part of the method.
- Data: the CiSE cohort template requires raw data at submission, cited in the text as Dataset 1, 2, ...
